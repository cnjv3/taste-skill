# Surface: Casa Miga home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Platform: web. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment; the client's reference mock rides to the finish review as the critique reference).

Audience and action: a nearby visitor on a phone decides whether to come. Actions, in order: open the menu, find the place, check hours, contact, follow on Instagram.

Proof and content: client copy and menu from the brief; photography is the client's mock imagery, extracted, cleaned and upscaled, labelled as placeholder. No claims beyond the brief.

Constraints: pinned palette and type families; placeholders for address, hours, phone, email, maps, Instagram, visibly marked; nothing falsely functional; prefers-reduced-motion honoured.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One sunlit Mediterranean morning told at breakfast pace. The page keeps returning to the same table: the cup is poured, filled and steams as you scroll. It refuses the café template of a centred cup on cream, a headline and three equal cards for coffee, pastries and community.

OWN-WORLD: Crema limewash and linen ground (#F5F0E7, #FBF9F4) with arena hairlines; one café oscuro chapter (#30241E) for the ritual and the closing footer; terracotta (#C77857 family) only where a hand touches: primary pill, selected tab, seal ink; olive (#687454) for drawn sprigs. Cormorant Garamond uppercase display with an italic second voice; Manrope for function, tracked caps on buttons and nav only. 1px pill buttons, a stamped circular seal, line-drawn olive sprigs, side-lit warm photography of stone, ceramic and flour.

STORY: feel the morning (hero), believe the care (philosophy), see what is offered and what it costs (carta), live the ritual (pinned scroll), want the room (nuestra casa), know how to get there (visítanos).

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed photo of the table, window light on the left, cup and croissant filling the right two thirds; nav row on top (logo left, four links, outlined "Ven a vernos" right); two-line headline in the left half at about 6rem, roman then italic; two-line subcopy; terracotta primary "Descubrir la carta" beside the outlined "Conocer nuestra casa"; seal below; scroll cue bottom left; live steam over the cup. Phone: text on the light upper half, cup and croissant filling the lower half.

FORM: brief-pinned editorial café spread from the client mock; no concept roll was run because the brief pins palette, type, copy and composition. Signature interaction: the ritual, a pinned scroll that pushes the camera into the pour, cross-dissolves to the filled cup, forms the crema and lets steam rise (photo sequence plus WebGL steam), stepping through Elegir, Preparar, Servir, Disfrutar. Second moment: the croissant macro push-in with a travelling light sweep in El obrador. seed: none (brief-pinned direction; roll not run)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
