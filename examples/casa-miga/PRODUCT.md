# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Inferred from the client brief ("Prompt maestro definitivo, Casa Miga") and its reference mock. The brief asked not to be interviewed on secondary details, so no interview round ran. Every fact below comes from the brief; nothing was added.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with GSAP + ScrollTrigger vendored locally for the two scroll-scrubbed narratives. No framework and no build step, because the site is one page plus two legal stubs and the brief asks for a simple, maintainable structure when there is no existing project.

## Users

People nearby deciding where to have coffee or breakfast, mostly on a phone: checking what the place is like, what is on the menu and roughly what it costs, when it is open and how to get there. A second audience is the owner, who must be able to edit prices, hours and links without a developer.

## Product Purpose

CASA MIGA is a fictional Mediterranean artisan café: specialty coffee, slow-fermented bakery, and time to enjoy them. The site must make a visitor want to go, then give them the menu, the hours and the way there.

## Positioning

"El placer de ir más despacio." Emotional luxury, not cold exclusivity: natural materials, excellent product, everyday beauty, personal hospitality. A Mediterranean independent café crossed with a contemporary bakery, an editorial food magazine and a ceramics boutique.

## Operating Context

Morning light through a window, a ceramic cup, a fresh croissant. The visit is the conversion: consult the menu, find the place, check the hours, contact, follow on Instagram.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Menu (from the brief, prices fictional and editable): Café (Espresso 2,20; Americano 2,50; Cappuccino 3,20; Flat White 3,40; Latte 3,50; Café de filtro 3,80), Panadería (Croissant de mantequilla 2,80; Pain au chocolat 3,20; Roll de canela 3,50; Focaccia artesanal 4,50), Dulces (Tarta de temporada 4,80; Bizcocho de limón 3,80; Cookie de chocolate 3,20), Desayunos (Tostada de masa madre con tomate 5,50; Yogur con fruta de temporada y granola 6,50; Tostada de aguacate 7,50).
- Undecided: address, opening hours, phone, email, Google Maps link, Instagram account. They stay as marked placeholders until the owner supplies them.
- Must not claim: daily availability, ingredients, provenance, certifications, reviews, awards, customer numbers or statistics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: CASA MIGA. Line: "El placer de ir más despacio." Secondary lines, used sparingly: "Un buen café. Algo recién horneado. Y tiempo para disfrutarlo.", "Pequeños rituales, hechos con cariño.", "El día sabe mejor cuando empieza sin prisa."
- Palette pinned by the brief: crema #F5F0E7, blanco cálido #FBF9F4, terracota #C77857, verde oliva #687454, marrón café #49352B, café oscuro #30241E, arena #DED2C0. Predominantly light and warm; no pure black, no neon, no purple gradients.
- Type pinned by the brief: an editorial serif (preference Cormorant Garamond) for headlines and a clean sans (preference Manrope) for function.
- Voice: Spanish, warm, unhurried, never salesy.
- Reference mock supplied by the client (desktop + phone composition, section order and copy).

## Evidence on Hand

- No real photography of the venue or products. The only imagery is the client's reference mock, whose photographs are AI-generated illustrations. They may be used as placeholders, labelled as such, and must be replaced by a real shoot before launch. Never present them as the real venue.
- No reviews, press, awards or figures exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Slowness is the product: pacing, motion and copy should feel unhurried, never sluggish.
2. Appetite first: photography and texture carry the page; chrome stays out of the way.
3. Honest hospitality: unverified data is visibly marked, and nothing pretends to work when it does not.
4. The phone is the main door: menu, hours and directions must be effortless on a small screen.
